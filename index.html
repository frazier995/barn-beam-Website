FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers about custom laser engraving, UV printing, quotes, and shipping.

What services do you offer?

We provide custom laser engraving and full-color UV printing for signs, hat patches, drinkware, personalized gifts, business branding, and fully custom projects on wood, metal, acrylic, leather, glass, fabric, and more.

How do I get a quote?

Use the Request a Quote form, email ltgallman@barn-beam.com, or call (816) 408-0675. Include your design, material, quantity, and timeline — we typically reply within 1–2 business days.

Do you ship finished orders?

Yes. Pieces can be packed and shipped, or you can arrange local pickup. Single pieces and small production runs are both welcome.

What materials can you engrave or print on?

Common options include wood, leather, acrylic, metal, glass, fabric, drinkware, hat patches, and other hard goods suited to laser or UV. Not sure which process fits? Describe the project and we’ll recommend materials and production.

Where are you located?

We’re at 1500 N Lake City Valley Rd, Buckner, MO 64016 (Kansas City metro). Visits are by appointment, with local pickup or shipping. Get directions on Google or contact us to schedule.
